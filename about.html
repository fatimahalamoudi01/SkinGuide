About SkinGuide

Our idea | The problem | Benefits | Our team

Our Idea

SkinGuide started from a question we hear all the time: "Which product should I buy for my skin?" Shops are full of skincare products, and every advertisement says its product is the best.

So we built SkinGuide: a simple website that explains the five skin types, shows which products suit each one and helps you build a small daily routine.

The Problem We Solve

  1. Many people do not know their skin type, so they buy products that make their skin worse.
  2. Skincare information online is spread over many websites and is often written to sell.
  3. People waste money on products they do not need.

Benefits of SkinGuide

  • Simple: every skin type is explained with a picture.
  • Personal: suggestions for your own skin type.
  • Saves money: you buy only what your skin needs.
  • Free: works on any device.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I identify my skin type?

You can identify your skin type by observing how your skin feels after cleansing.

2. Why is moisturizing important?

Moisturizing helps keep your skin hydrated and supports the skin barrier.

3. Should I use sunscreen every day?

Yes, daily sunscreen helps protect your skin from harmful UV rays.
