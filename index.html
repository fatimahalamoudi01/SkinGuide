Skin Types at a Glance

The five skin types
Skin type Signs Helpful ingredients
How it looks How it feels
Oily Shiny, large pores Greasy by midday Niacinamide, salicylic acid
Combination Shiny forehead and nose Dry cheeks
Normal Even, small pores Comfortable Vitamin C
Dry Dull, flaky areas Tight after washing Ceramides, hyaluronic acid
Sensitive Red areas Itchy or burning
Every skin type needs sunscreen every day.
